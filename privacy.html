Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. In short: we collect only what the game needs to work, we don’t show ads, and we don’t sell your data.

Who we are

The data controller is Elvoren OÜ, [registered address], Estonia (registry code [code]). Contact: developer@elvoren.ee.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
A random player ID (guest account)When you first open the gameTo keep your farm on our server
Game progress (farm, items, coins, level, quests)While you playTo run the game and prevent cheating
Your farm name and chosen profile pictureWhen you change themTo show them to your neighbours and co-op members
Neighbours, co-op membership, visits and likesWhen you use these featuresTo make the social features work
Your Apple or Google account ID (and the email address the provider shares)Only if you link your accountSo you can open your farm on other devices
Error reports (error message, app version, device type, browser details)If the game runs into an errorTo find and fix bugs

We don’t access your location, contacts, photos or microphone. We don’t show ads or track you for advertising, and we don’t sell your data.

Notifications

“Your crops are ready” reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. They are sent only if you allow them, and you can turn them off in the game under the Farmhouse.

Where and how long we keep data

Data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

  • Game data: kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Error reports: deleted after 30 days.
  • Farm visit records: deleted after 30 days.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all data linked to it at any time in the game: Farmhouse → Privacy and account → Delete my account and farm.

Under the GDPR (and, for players in Turkey, the KVKK) you can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or object to how we use it. Write to developer@elvoren.ee. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children under [age, matching the store age rating], and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll publish the new version on this page and announce important changes in the game.

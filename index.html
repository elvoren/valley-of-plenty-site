Your little farm by the sea

Olive trees, golden wheat and a whitewashed village on the hill: a small farm is waiting for you in a sunny valley on the Aegean coast. Plant, harvest, cook and trade, a few relaxing minutes at a time.

Plant and harvest

Wheat, corn, carrots, tomatoes, strawberries, lavender and more. Swipe across your fields to sow and gather in one go.

Care for your animals

Hens lay eggs, cows give milk and sheep grow fluffy wool. Watch them wander around the farm.

Bake, churn and weave

Mill flour, bake bread, make cheese and jam, weave fabric. Fill the villagers’ orders and earn coins.

Grow your valley

Buy new land from the riverbank to the hills, upgrade your buildings and decorate every corner.

Trade at the market

Sell your goods at your market stall and pick up what you need from your neighbours.

Play at your own pace

No rush and no penalties. Drop by for a few minutes a day; your farm will be waiting.

Better together

The village square has seen better days. Join a co-op, help each other out and restore it, stage by stage, until the lanterns are lit for the festival.

  • Join a co-op and send help where it’s needed
  • Restore the village square together, step by step
  • Visit your neighbours’ farms and leave a like

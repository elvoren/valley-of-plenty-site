Help & Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Contact us

Write to developer@elvoren.ee. Tell us your farm name and your neighbour code (Neighbours panel in the game) so we can find your farm. We usually reply within two working days.

How do I keep my farm if I change phones?

Open the Farmhouse in the game and link your Apple or Google account. On your new phone, install the game and link the same account: your farm comes with you.

I lost my farm. What can I do?

If you linked an Apple or Google account, link the same account again in the Farmhouse and choose your old farm. If you played as a guest without linking, write to us with your farm name and neighbour code and we’ll do our best to help.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

In the Farmhouse, under Notifications. You can also set quiet hours, when the game will never notify you.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your phone’s language. To pick another one, open the Farmhouse and choose it under Language.

Deleting your account and data

In the game: Farmhouse → Privacy and account → Delete my account and farm. Your account and all data linked to it are deleted right away.

If you can’t open the game anymore, write to developer@elvoren.ee with the subject “Delete my account” and your farm name or neighbour code. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleted: your farm, items, progress, farm name and picture, co-op membership, neighbours, visits and likes, and the link to your Apple or Google account. Error reports are removed automatically after 30 days.
